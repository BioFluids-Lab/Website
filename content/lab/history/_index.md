      text: >-
        The laboratory has been part of the School of Mechanical Engineering since
        the mid-nineties. These are the points at which its direction changed.

  - block: bf-timeline
    content:
      items:
        - date: "1994"
          title: Laboratory founded
          text: Established within the Fluids Section of the School of Mechanical Engineering, initially focused on classical internal flow measurement.
        - date: "2001"
          title: First cardiovascular flow rig
          text: A steady-flow arterial model bench is built, marking the shift toward biological flows.
        - date: "2008"
          title: PIV capability installed
          text: Planar particle image velocimetry replaces point-wise anemometry as the laboratory's primary measurement method.
        - date: "2013"
          title: Microfluidics group formed
          text: A soft lithography bench is added and the first lab-on-a-chip devices are cast in house.
        - date: "2017"
          title: Programmable pulsatile loop
          text: Waveform-controlled pumping enables physiological and pathological conditions to be reproduced repeatably.
        - date: "2021"
          title: Stereoscopic and tomographic imaging
          text: Three-component velocimetry brings the laboratory into volumetric measurement.
        - date: "2024"
          title: Device testing programme
          text: Standards-referenced in vitro qualification begins, in collaboration with clinical and industrial partners.
