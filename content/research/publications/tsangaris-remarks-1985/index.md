abstract: Some interesting aspects of the viscous flow due to an oscillating pressure
  gradient along a straight, circular pipe with rigid walls are discussed. The oscillating
  velocity amplitude and phase difference angle obtained in analytical closed forms
  have the known boundary layer characteristics. The phase difference angle between
  velocity and pressure gradient involves a discontinuity at the wall for large values
  of the dimensionless frequency and it can be greater than - 90 degree as well as
  smaller.
