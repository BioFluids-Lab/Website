abstract: The influence of artificial dissipation schemes on the accuracy and stability
  of the numerical solution of compressible flow is extensively examined. Using an
  implicit central difference factored scheme, an improved form of artificial dissipation
  is introduced which highly reduces the errors due to numerical viscosity. A function
  of the local Mach number is used to scale the amount of numerical damping added
  into the solution according to the character of the flow in several flow regimes.
  The resulting scheme is validated through several inviscid flow test cases. Copyright
