abstract: The flow field around a time-dependent 3-D asymmetric tube constriction
  was studied and comparisons were made with the corresponding 2-D case. Both experimental
  (LDV-PIV) and numerical (finite volume) tools were employed for Re textless 1000,
  St textless 0.3 and 50% maximum passage reduction. The basic features of the flow
  were 3-D separation and reattachment, vortex generation especially after the middle
  of the cycle (max. constriction), and vortex destruction before the end of the cycle.
  The snake-type shape of the streamlines, known from the 2-D case, was not observed
  on the symmetry plane of the examined flow due to the secondary flow action. © 2003
