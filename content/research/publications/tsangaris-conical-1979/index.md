abstract: Using a transonic approximation, the paper deals analytically with the supersonic
  flow around slender yawed circular cones with attached shock wave. This represents
  an extension of the axisymmetric problem calculated by Oswatitsch and Sjoedin in
  1954. According to a paper by Oswatitsch and Tsangaris, the solutions depend on
  two parameters of similarity. Results are compared with a pole-dipole approximation,
  the acoustic approximation and the data of the Sims tables.
