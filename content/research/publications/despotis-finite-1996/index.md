abstract: The extrudate swell phenomenon is analysed by solving, simultaneously, the
  Navier-Stokes equations along with the continuity equation by means of a finite
  volume method. In this work, the planar jet flows of incompressible viscous Newtonian
  and power-law fluids for Reynolds numbers as high as 75 are simulated. The method
  uses the velocity components and pressure as the primitive variables and employs
  an unstructured triangular grid and triangular or polygonal control volume for each
  separate variable. The numerical results show good agreement with previously reported
  experimental and numerical results. Shear thickening results in an increase in swelling
  ratio, while the introduction of surface tension results in a describes in swelling
  ratio.
