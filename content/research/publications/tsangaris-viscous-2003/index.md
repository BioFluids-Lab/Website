abstract: The motion of a Newtonian liquid contained in an angulated tube, which oscillates
  under the effect of external stimulation and gravity, was studied. It was experimentally
  established that the one-dimensional theory developed in the present study predicts
  satisfactorily the meniscus position, for frequencies of oscillation below 0.5 Hz
  and tube radius above 2.5 mm.
