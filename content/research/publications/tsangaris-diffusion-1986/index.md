publication: '*Zeitschrift fur angewandte Mathematik und Mechanik*'
abstract: Physiological pumps, like heart, produce flows by alternate contraction
  and expansion of the vessel. These systems can be modeled by a semi-infinite pipe
  with one end closed, which prevents only axial motion of the fluid leaving radial
  motion completely unrestricted. In the present study the problem of the advection
  of a property in the two-dimensional unsteady flow field produced by expansion or
  contraction of the channel walls is studied analytically.
