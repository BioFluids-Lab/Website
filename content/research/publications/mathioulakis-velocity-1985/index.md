publication: '*Calif. State Univ*'
abstract: Steady and unsteady velocity components over a backward facing circular
  arc are measured by a 2 component laser Doppler velocimetry. A periodic disturbance
  is added to the mean flow and the response of unsteady separation is investigated.
  Based on the measured quantities, the instantaneous values of the terms of the Navier
  Stokes equations are calculated. Special attention is given to the distribution
  and the flux of vorticity. (A)
