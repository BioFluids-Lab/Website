abstract: Fully developed parallel flow in straight pipes allows analytical solution
  of the viscous flow equations. Incompressible newtonian fluids flowing steadily
  in a straight elliptical pipe have been studied a long time ago by Boussinesq. Unsteady
  viscous flow investigations are more recent. The present theory is required in order
  to provide local velocities for pulsatile flow in a long elliptical pipe.
