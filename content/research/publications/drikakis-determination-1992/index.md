abstract: In the present paper the determination of an inviscid ideal non-dissociated
  gas flow into an axisymmetric convergent-divergent nozzle is obtained using high
  order of accuracy methods, greater than the second order of accuracy, for the discretization
  of the compressible Euler equations. Three different methods are used. The spatial
  accuracy of the methods is up to fourth order while comparison with second order
  of accuracy solution is presented. A modern implicit Newton-type method leads to
  fast convergence to steady state solution. Comparison of the computational results
  with the corresponding experimental data, confirm the reliability of the present
  methods.
