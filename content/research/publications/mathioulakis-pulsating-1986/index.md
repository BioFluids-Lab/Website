abstract: The work reported in this paper is a continuation of an experimental effort
  on unsteady laminar separation. In earlier reports the author discuss the problem
  of laminar separation over a curved surface with particular attention to the immediate
  neighborhood of separation. In the present paper the authors report on an extension
  of a work which includes detailed measurements near, as well as upstream and downstream
  of separation, all the way to the point of stagnation and beyond the downstream
  end of the body, into the wake. The data presented here therefore offer a complete
  and detailed picture of unsteady separating flow over a bluff body, readily available
  for comparison with numerical calculations.
