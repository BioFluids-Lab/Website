abstract: In this paper an implicit fractional step method for the solution of the
  two‐dimensional, time‐dependent, incompressible Navier‐Stokes equations is presented.
  The current method was developed for use on an unstructured grid made up of triangles.
  The basic principles of this method are that the evaluation of the time evolution
  is split into intermediate steps and that for the spatial discretization of the
  flow equations a finite volume discretization on an unstructured triangular mesh
  is used. The present approach has been used to simulate viscous, laminar flows for
  various Reynolds numbers in test cases such as a backward‐facing step, a square
  cavity and a channel with wavy boundaries. Copyright © 1995 John Wiley & Sons, Ltd
