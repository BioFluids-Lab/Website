abstract: Two-component LDV measurements are obtained over an ellipse at an angle
  of attack. Detailed information is provided for the stagnation region, the two separation
  regions and the two free shear layers. Steady and unsteady flow measurements are
  presented. Periodic disturbances in the oncoming stream are introduced and ensemble-averaged
  unsteady data are obtained. An integral picture of laminar flow over a lifting body
  with no sharp edges is thus presented, which is readily available for comparison
  with asymptotic or numerical calculations. © 1989, Cambridge University Press. All
