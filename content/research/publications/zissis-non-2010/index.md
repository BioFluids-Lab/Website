abstract: The flow behavior of four non newtonian shear thinning fluids, namely Walburn-Schneck,
  Herschel-Bulkley, Quemada and Casson were studied numerically in a straight circular
  tube as well as in a 75% axisymmetric stenosis under steady and unsteady inlet flow
  conditions. Reynolds varied from 25 to 150, Strouhal from 0.05 to 1 and Womersley
  number from 2.8 to 12.53. Comparisons were performed between the models and the
  Newtonian one. In steady flow cases, the non Newtonian behaviour becomes evident
  with reducing Re whereas in unsteady cases, the flow patterns are dependent on Re,
  Str and the fluid model. © 2010 IEEE.
