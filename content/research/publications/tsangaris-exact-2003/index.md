abstract: The Navier-Stokes equations have been solved in order to obtain an analytical
  solution of the fully developed laminar flow in a duct having a cross section of
  a right-angled, isosceles triangle. We obtained a solution for the case of oscillating
  pressure gradient flow. The pulsating flow is obtained by the superposition of the
  steady and oscillating pressure gradient solutions.
