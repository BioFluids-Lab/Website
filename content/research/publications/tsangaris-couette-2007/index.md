abstract: In the present paper the flow of a Bingham fluid between two parallel porous
  walls is studied. One of the walls moves with constant velocity parallel to the
  other, which is fixed, while a longitudinal pressure gradient exists, as well as
  a transverse flow field due the porosity of the walls. An exact analytical solution
  is given for the u-velocity field, which has four different forms depending on the
  values of the three dimensionless parameters, which are the Bingham, Couette and
  Reynolds numbers. © 2007 Elsevier B.V. All rights reserved.
