abstract: In this paper the viscous flow due to an oscillating pressure gradient along
  a straight circular annular pipe with rigid walls is discussed. The amplitude and
  the phase difference of the oscillating velocity are obtained in analytical closed
  form. The discussion is limited to two dimensionless parameters, the Wormersley
  parameter and the ratio between the radii of the annulus. The limiting cases of
  steady flow inside an annulus and of oscillating flow in a tube of circular cross
  section are also given.
