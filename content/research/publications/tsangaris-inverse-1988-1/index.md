abstract: In this study a solution for the problem of pulsating flow in rigid tubes
  is described. The method is applied to the case of the known wall shear stress wave
  form as opposed to J. R. Womersley's solution, where the pressure gradient along
  the pipe wall was the known quantity. The solution provides the flow rate and the
  pressure gradient as well as the instantaneous axial velocity profiles. The method
  is based on the same governing equation used by Womersley which is the momentum
  equation along the center-line of the pipe. The equation is derived assuming only
  the axial velocity components to be non-zero. 4 Refs.
