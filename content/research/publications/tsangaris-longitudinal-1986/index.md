abstract: The dispersion of a diffusing substance along a channel or pipe is augmented
  if the channel walls (resp. pipe wall) are moving in the lateral direction. The
  fluid in the present theoretical investigation is supposed to be inviscid. The longitudinal
  dispersion enhancement is evaluated analytically and the expressions for various
  movements of the walls are given in simple formulas. © 1986 Birkhäuser Verlag.
