abstract: An investigation of abdominal aortic aneurysms (AAAs) is performed based
  on medical Computed Tomography (CT) images. The images are transformed into 3D surface
  models to simulate deformation behavior of AAAs and estimate the rupture risk. The
  finite element method (FEM) with nonlinear hyperelastic Yeoh material model and
  the linear model for comparison are used. The aorta has been modelled with the shell
  theory, with appropriate adjustments to take into account the intraluminal thrombus.
  Numerical results are obtained for the maximum value of the von Mises stresses and
  strains. The introduced techniques can provide sufficient estimates for the displacements
  at inaccessible points, like virtual sensors, which can be used for rupture prediction
  within an integrated artificial intelligence tool. © The Author(s) 2026.
