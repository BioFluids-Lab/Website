abstract: The study presents a generalized analytical solution of the laminar, oscillatory,
  creeping flow of an incompressible Newtonian fluid in a porous circular pipe with
  spatiotemporally periodic suction/injection at the wall. The analytical solution
  is examined for a variety of values of the dimensionless parameters, namely the
  Womersley number and the dimensionless suction/injection number. © 2022 by the authors.
