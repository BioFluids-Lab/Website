abstract: In the present paper a perturbation method is developed in order to study
  viscous, laminar flow over a small amplitude wavy solid boundary. The wall is transformed
  into a straight solid boundary in order to simplify the use of boundary conditions
  of the problem on the wall. The flow field is calculated numerically up to first
  perturbation order. separation of the flow is included. © 1986 Springer-Verlag.
