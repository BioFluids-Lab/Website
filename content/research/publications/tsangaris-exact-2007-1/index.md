abstract: This paper presents an extension of the exact solution of the steady laminar
  axisymmetric flow in a straight pipe of circular cross section with porous wall,
  given by R.M. Terrill, to the case of unsteady wall injection and/or suction. The
  cases of the pulsating parabolic profile and of the developed pulsating flow are
  investigated as examples. The pulsating flow in porous ducts has many applications
  in biomedical engineering and in other engineering areas. © 2006 Elsevier B.V. All
