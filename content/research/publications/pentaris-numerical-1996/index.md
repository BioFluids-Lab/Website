abstract: In this paper an implicit projection method for the solution of the two-dimensional,
  time-dependent, incompressible Navier - Stokes equations is presented. The basic
  principle of this method is that the evaluation of the time evolution is split into
  intermediate steps. The computational method is based on the approximate factorization
  technique. The coupled approach is used to link the equations of motion and the
  turbulence model equations. The standard k - ∈ turbulence model is used. The current
  methodology, which has been tested extensively for steady problems, is now applied
  for the numerical simulation of unsteady flows. Several cases were tested, such
  as plane or axisymmetric channels, a backward-facing step, a square cavity and an
  axisymmetric stenosis.
