        - name: Cardiovascular & Hemodynamics
          mode: vortex
          seed: 3
          caption: Velocity field in a compliant aortic arch model, systolic peak.
          paragraphs:
            - >-
              Flow in arteries, heart valves and stents, measured under physiological
              pulsatile conditions. We build compliant silicone models from patient
              geometry, drive them with a programmable pump, and map the velocity field
              with planar and stereoscopic PIV.
            - >-
              Current questions concern wall shear stress distribution downstream of
              stenoses, and how leaflet design changes the washout of the sinus region.
          topics: [Heart valves, Stenosis, Wall shear, Aneurysm]
          links:
            - text: Related projects

            - text: Publications
              url: /research/publications/

        - name: Microfluidics & Lab-on-a-Chip
          mode: channel
          seed: 11
          caption: Droplet train in a 200 micron serpentine channel.
          paragraphs:
            - >-
              Miniaturised platforms for cell sorting, blood analogue rheology, and
              point-of-care diagnostics. Devices are designed in house, cast in PDMS,
              and characterised with micro-PIV and high-speed imaging.
            - >-
              The recurring difficulty is that blood stops behaving like a continuum at
              these scales, so a large part of the work is establishing what a valid
              analogue fluid actually is.
          topics: [Cell sorting, Droplets, Rheology, PDMS]
          links:
            - text: Related projects
              url: /research/projects/
            - text: Publications
              url: /research/publications/

        - name: Biomedical Device Design & Testing
          mode: wake
          seed: 27
          caption: Simulated wake behind a prototype cannula tip.
          paragraphs:
            - >-
              Design, prototyping and in vitro qualification of implants and
              extracorporeal circuits. Work runs from early concept geometry through
              printed prototypes to standards-referenced bench testing.
            - >-
              We collaborate with clinical groups on cannula and oxygenator geometry,
              where hemolysis risk and pressure drop pull the design in opposite
              directions.
          topics: [Prototyping, Hemolysis, ISO 5840, ECMO]
          links:
            - text: Related projects

            - text: Publications
              url: /research/publications/

        - name: Flow Imaging & Diagnostics
          mode: shear
          seed: 41
          caption: Vorticity from a tomographic PTV reconstruction.
          paragraphs:
            - >-
              Method development for PIV, PTV and image-based velocimetry in optically
              difficult geometries. Curved compliant walls, refractive index mismatch
              and limited optical access are the normal condition in this field rather
              than the exception.
            - >-
              Recent effort has gone into uncertainty quantification, so that a measured
              velocity field carries an honest error estimate alongside it.
          topics: [PIV / PTV, Tomographic, Uncertainty, Index matching]
